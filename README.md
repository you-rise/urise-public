# URise

**The Stage for What's Next.**

URise is a product discovery and community platform for makers, indie hackers, and startup founders. It enables users to browse and discover newly launched products, launch and promote their own creations, and engage with a community of builders.

## Features

- **Product Discovery** — Browse trending and newly launched products in a card-based feed
- **Product Launches** — Launch and promote products with AI-generated summaries and rich media
- **Community Engagement** — Upvote, comment, and interact with product creators
- **Weekly Battles** — Gamified product competitions with leaderboards
- **Community Forum** — Discussion threads for the maker community
- **Creator Dashboard** — Analytics and management tools for product creators
- **Admin Panel** — Moderation and platform management
- **Badges & Leaderboards** — Gamification and recognition system
- **AI-Powered Tools** — Google Gemini integration for SEO optimization and product summaries
- **Blog, Changelog & Roadmap** — Content and updates from the team
- **Subscription Tiers** — Paid plans via Polar.sh (Merchant of Record)

## Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 15 (App Router, TypeScript)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Radix UI
- **Database & Auth:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage, RLS)
- **Payments:** Polar.sh
- **AI:** Google Gemini via Genkit
- **Email:** Resend
- **Rate Limiting & Cache:** Upstash Redis
- **Rich Text:** Tiptap
- **Analytics:** Vercel Analytics, Sentry
- **Testing:** Jest, Testing Library
- **Deployment:** Vercel, Firebase App Hosting
